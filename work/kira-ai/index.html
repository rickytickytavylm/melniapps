Kira AI

Intelligence built into the product.

A multipurpose AI assistant for digital platforms and organizations. It handles initial consultations, helps people navigate programs, and supports students through course material.

Challenge

People arriving at an educational or care platform rarely know which format fits their situation. A generic support widget only adds friction. The goal was to build a calm, reliable assistant that understands context, answers substantive questions, and guides each person toward a clear next step.

How it works in practice

  • Private, around-the-clock conversations in multiple languages without rigid intake forms.
  • Thoughtful matching to the right program or consultation based on the actual situation described.
  • Guided work with course materials and assignments, with a clean handoff to a human coordinator when needed.

Engineering

The web application and Telegram bot share a common backend and domain layer. The language model is grounded in the organization's programs, rules, and boundaries rather than left to improvise. One live deployment powers Dr. Shurov's School, where Dr. Vasily Shurov is the project partner while the platform architecture and AI engineering are built by MELNI APPS.

Delivery

Kira AI is in active production on the web at kira-ai.online and on Telegram.
