04 / Approach

Understand. Build. Verify. Evolve.

A product earns its keep after the first release, once real people begin to rely on it.

  1. Step 01

    Explore

    Understand the domain and strip away what is unnecessary before writing code.

  2. Step 02

    Build

    Deliver the whole stack: from database schema and API to the final interface.

  3. Step 03

    Validate

    Test against real usage, edge cases, and operational load.

  4. Step 04

    Evolve

    Refine what matters as the product grows in production.
