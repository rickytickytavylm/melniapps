Fides et Ratio

A digital home for a community.

A mobile platform for Russian-speaking Catholics: over five hundred audio recordings on the Catechism, daily readings, homilies, live Radio Maria broadcast, and an AI study companion.

View on the App Store (RU)
Product type
Mobile media platform

iOS
Role

Stack
Mobile client, backend, audio streaming, AI

Challenge

Prayers, homilies, Catechism lectures, and Catholic radio had long lived across scattered websites and archives. The goal was to bring them together into a single, dignified mobile application designed for daily listening and reading.

Inside the app

  • Over 500 audio lessons on the Catechism of the Catholic Church with automatic progress saving, alongside homilies and audio prayers.
  • Live Radio Maria streaming with broadcast schedules, short vertical videos, and full-length lectures.
  • Daily quotes from the saints, Church news, and an AI assistant grounded in Catholic teaching.

Engineering

iOS application with background audio playback, video streaming, light and dark themes, a content management backend, and an AI assistant connected to doctrinal sources.

Delivery

Free and ad-free, published on the Russian App Store, with an English edition in preparation.
