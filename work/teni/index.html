Teni Championship

Beyond the event website.

A web system for a stage and pole-art championship in Moscow: a theatrical public site, online registration with video submissions, and an internal CRM for the organizing committee.

Visit website
Product type
Event platform and CRM
Platform
Web
Role
Design, backend, CRM
Stack
Public site, intake flow, applicant database

Challenge

A national stage-art competition at the Golden Ring Theatre needed more than a poster page. It had to present categories, judging criteria, and the 400,000 ₽ prize fund clearly, collect applications with qualification videos, and give the organizers a structured workspace to review every entry.

How it works

  • A dark, stage-lit public website detailing the schedule, rules, categories, and judging panel.
  • A step-by-step registration flow for performers submitting qualification videos.
  • A private CRM where the organizing team reviews submissions, tracks statuses, and manages participant records.

Engineering

A unified build combining a responsive public frontend, backend API, applicant database, and an internal management interface.

Delivery

The platform is live and accepting entries for the November 2026 championship in Moscow.
