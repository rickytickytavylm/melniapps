Loza

A community, built as a product.

A dedicated web platform for parents of teenagers: psychologist-led lectures, a curated media library, member discussions, and an integrated AI assistant in a calm, coherent interface.

Visit website
Product type
Community web platform
Platform
Web
Role
End-to-end engineering
Stack
Member accounts, media library, chat, CRM, AI

Challenge

Member communities often end up scattered across third-party tools: videos on one service, chat in a messenger, access managed by hand. Loza needed a single, quiet environment where parents could read, watch, and talk without friction.

What members get

  • A unified club feed and a structured media library of lectures, practices, and film discussions.
  • Direct conversation with other parents and club psychologists inside the platform.
  • Personal accounts with saved progress and immediate access to an in-product AI assistant.

Engineering

Full-stack web application with Yandex OAuth, membership and access control, media delivery, real-time community rooms, AI integration, and an internal CRM for the editorial team.

Delivery

The platform is live in production at lozapsy.help.
