Kira AI

Intelligence built into the product.

An AI assistant designed to work inside digital services, helping users find answers, navigate learning materials, and interact with products.

Open the productTelegram bot
Product type
AI product
Platform
Web, Telegram
Role
Product engineering
Technologies
Model integration, conversational flows, backend services

Challenge

The assistant had to live inside a real service, not as a separate chat sitting next to it. People needed answers, a way through the material, and a clear next step.

Experience

  • Ask a question and get a contextual reply.
  • Move through learning material without leaving the product.
  • Reach a person when the conversation should continue outside the chat.

Engineering

The work covers model integration, contextual replies, conversational flows, backend services, and logic that belongs to this product. Dr. Shurov's School is one live deployment. Dr. Shurov is the partner, not the builder of the AI.

Delivery

Kira AI is available on the web and as a Telegram bot.
