Leo Recovery

Recovery, one day at a time.

An original mobile app for people overcoming addiction and for their families: a structured course of over thirty chapters, an emotion journal, guided audio practices, and a 24/7 AI companion.

View on the App Store#9 on Product Hunt
Product type
Original product
Platform
iOS and Android
Role
Concept, design, engineering
Stack
React Native, Expo, backend services, AI

Challenge

Recovery happens between appointments, in ordinary hours when cravings or anxiety hit. People in recovery and their loved ones needed a calm, private tool they could open at any hour without judgment.

Inside the app

  • Over thirty chapters of practical theory on addiction and codependency, paired with speaker stories and short daily videos.
  • A sobriety counter and an emotion journal that helps spot recurring triggers and patterns.
  • Audio practices for acute moments and an AI assistant tuned to the context of recovery.

Engineering

Cross-platform mobile application built with React Native and Expo, persistent user state, audio playback, push notifications, and a secure backend connecting the app to the language model.

Delivery

Published on the App Store and featured as the #9 Product of the Day on Product Hunt.
